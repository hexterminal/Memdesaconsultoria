Specialized Financial Assistance

Organization, control, and support for daily financial routines, with processes adapted to the company’s reality.

Main solutions: accounts payable and receivable management; financial reconciliation and document organization; cash flow control and updates; billing and accountability reports; spreadsheet development and operational support.

Financial Consulting and Management

Process structuring and decision support based on reliable information.

We provide financial and budget planning, support for management control, performance indicators, managerial cost and margin analysis, support for pricing, financial diagnostics, control reorganization, managerial feasibility studies, and management reports.

Technology and Training

Tools and knowledge applied to strengthen financial management and improve team productivity.

We develop structured spreadsheets, dashboards, management reports, Power BI solutions, administrative automations, and information integration applied to financial management. We also provide training in cash flow, budgeting, costs, indicators, and information technology applied to financial management.

Public Contracts and Construction Financial Management

Support for the economic, financial, budgetary, administrative, and documentary aspects of contracts and construction projects.

Solutions include economic and financial analyses for price adjustments, contract cost reviews and rebalancing, supporting calculations, financial analysis of procurement notices and contracts, disbursement flows, budget control, and management reports. We do not provide legal analysis, engineering design, technical measurements, inspection, engineering execution, or technical responsibility.
