Specialized Financial Assistance

Organization, control, and support for daily financial routines, with processes adapted to the company’s reality.

Main solutions: accounts payable and receivable management; financial reconciliation and document organization; cash flow control and updates; billing and accountability reports; spreadsheet development and operational support.

Financial Consulting and Management

Process structuring and decision support based on reliable information.

We work with financial and budget planning; controlling and performance indicators; cost, margin, and pricing analysis; financial diagnostics; process restructuring; feasibility studies; and management reports.

Technology and Training

Tools and knowledge applied to strengthen financial management and increase team productivity.

We develop standardized spreadsheets, dashboards, management reports, Power BI solutions, control automation, and information integration. We also provide training in cash flow, budgeting, costs, indicators, and information technology applied to financial management.

Public Contracts and Construction Financial Management

Support for the economic, financial, budgetary, administrative, and documentary aspects of contracts and construction projects.

Solutions include price adjustments, contract revisions, economic and financial rebalancing, calculation memoranda, financial analysis of bids and contracts, disbursement flows, budget control, and financial reports. We do not provide legal services, engineering design, technical measurements, inspection, or engineering execution.
