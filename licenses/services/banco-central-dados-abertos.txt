Use on the website:
Current value and recent historical chart.

2. IPCA accumulated in the year

Official indicator:
Índice Nacional de Preços ao Consumidor Amplo - IPCA

SGS series identifier:
433

Original series unit:
Monthly percentage variation

Website processing:
The website retrieves the monthly IPCA variations published for the
current calendar year and calculates the compounded year-to-date result.

Calculation:
[(1 + rate_1/100) × (1 + rate_2/100) × ... ×
(1 + latest_rate/100) - 1] × 100

Periodicity:
Monthly

API base:
https://api.bcb.gov.br/dados/serie/bcdata.sgs.433/dados

Displayed value:
Accumulated IPCA from January through the latest available month.

Chart:
Evolution of the compounded year-to-date IPCA.

Important note:
The displayed accumulated value is calculated by the website from the
official monthly series. It is not copied directly from a separate
12-month accumulated series.
