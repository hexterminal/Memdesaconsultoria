Essential concepts

Financial and contractual glossary

Cash flow

Record and forecast of cash inflows and outflows.

Working capital

Resources needed to maintain operations.

Contribution margin

Revenue minus variable costs and expenses.

Break-even point

Level at which revenue equals total costs.

Price adjustment

Periodic update of a price using the contractual index.

Contract cost review

Contract cost revision based on changes in cost components.

Economic and financial rebalance

Restoration of the contract’s original economic and financial conditions.

Supporting calculation

Document stating assumptions, formulas, indices, periods, and amounts.

KPI

Key performance indicator.

Dashboard

Visual panel of indicators and information.

Financial schedule

Timing of receipts and payments.

Business budget

Estimate of revenue, costs, expenses, and results.

Asset

Resource controlled by the business that may generate future economic benefits.

Liability

Present obligation that may require an outflow of resources.

Equity

Difference between an organization’s assets and liabilities.

Revenue

Value generated by sales, services, or other activities.

Fixed cost

Cost that tends to remain stable within a given activity range.

Variable cost

Cost that changes with the volume produced, sold, or delivered.

Operating expense

Expense related to administration, sales, and ongoing operations.

Gross profit

Result after deducting directly related costs from revenue.

Net profit

Final result after costs, expenses, taxes, and other charges.

Liquidity

Ability to convert assets into available funds to meet obligations.

Default

Failure to meet a financial obligation by its due date.

Accounts payable

Financial obligations the business must settle.

Accounts receivable

Amounts the business is entitled to receive from customers or third parties.

Bank reconciliation

Comparison of internal records with bank transactions to identify differences.

Financial provision

Amount reserved or recognized for a probable obligation or expense.

Emergency reserve

Funds held for unforeseen events and business continuity.

Debt level

Financial obligations in relation to repayment capacity.

Cash conversion cycle

Period between paying suppliers and collecting sales.

Operating cycle

Time from acquiring inputs to collecting the resulting sales.

Average payment period

Average time the business takes to pay suppliers and other obligations.

Average collection period

Average time needed to collect amounts from customers.

EBITDA

Operating result before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization.

ROI

Indicator comparing the gain obtained with the amount invested.

Return

Ability of an investment or operation to generate a return on the resources used.

Profit margin

Profit as a percentage of revenue over a defined period.

Markup

Factor applied to cost to support selling-price formation.

Average transaction value

Average value of sales or transactions over a defined period.

Budget versus actual

Comparison between planned amounts and amounts actually incurred.

Financial forecast

Estimate of future revenue, payments, balances, and funding needs.

Financial scenario

Set of assumptions used to assess possible future outcomes.

Sensitivity analysis

Assessment of how changes in key assumptions affect a forecast.

NPV

Present value of future cash flows discounted at a defined rate.

IRR

Rate at which the present value of investment inflows equals outflows.

Payback period

Estimated time needed to recover the initial investment.

Adjustment index

Reference used to update amounts under defined criteria and periods.

Base date

Reference date or period used for adjustments, calculations, and comparisons.

Contract amendment

Document that formalizes changes to the original contract conditions.

Traceability

Ability to identify the origin, changes, and history of information.

Business calendar

Weekly

Update accounts, balances, and due dates.

Monthly

Close the cash records and compare budget with actual results.

Quarterly

Review costs, prices, margins, and working capital.

Annually

Prepare the budget, targets, and scenarios.

Alerts and precautions

  • Avoid outdated spreadsheets or files with no identified source.
  • Do not send passwords or confidential documents through open forms.
  • Document assumptions, indices, periods, and sources.
  • Maintain backups and access controls.
