Meet Mem de Sá Ratio Consilium

The Mem de Sá Ratio Consilium is a company specializing in financial consulting and management, dedicated to helping organizations build more structured processes, more efficient controls, and safer decisions.

Our work combines financial analysis, information organization, management support, training, and technology applied to management, with solutions tailored to each client’s size, needs, and stage.

Important information

Frequently asked questions

Does Ratio Consilium serve companies of any size?

Each request is assessed according to the need, complexity, available information, and compatibility with the company’s professional scope.

Can services be provided remotely?

Yes. The service format is defined according to the scope, location, and client needs.

Can a specific service be contracted?

Yes. The work may cover a one-off request, a fixed-term project, or recurring support.

Does the company provide accounting services or investment advice?

No. Our work is administrative, managerial, and financial. It does not include regulated accounting services, independent audit, asset management, or individual investment recommendations.

Does financial management of construction projects include technical responsibility?

No. The work is limited to the contracted financial, budgetary, and administrative controls, without engineering, architecture, execution, inspection, or technical responsibility.

How are deadlines and fees defined?

After the request is assessed, a proposal is presented with scope, deliverables, timeframe, responsibilities, and commercial terms.
